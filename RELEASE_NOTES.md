# Relay IPTV 0.3.0

A complete redesign. Phones and tablets now look and feel like modern Android (Material 3 Expressive), and Android TV and Google TV get a proper TV interface (Material 3 for TV). It's faster too.

**Everywhere**
- New look: slate and amber colours, the Roboto Flex typeface, smooth spring animations
- Every tab remembers where you were: switch away and come back to the same place
- Faster start and smoother scrolling, even with very large playlists
- New players: a clean overlay with the programme, a progress bar and real buttons (pause, from the start, go live, audio & subtitles, save, channels)
- A loading indicator whenever a stream is buffering

**Phones and tablets**
- New For you: a big card for the channel you were watching, your other channels, continue watching, recently watched and added, and your categories
- Navigation bar: For you · Live · Guide · Library · Saved (a side rail on tablets and in landscape)
- Swipe between categories in Live and Library, seasons, and Saved; the Guide moves through time as you swipe
- Live keeps playing in a mini player while you browse (swipe it away to stop)
- Options open as bottom sheets, removals offer Undo, and Back works the Android way with predictive back

**Android TV**
- A menu down the left, reached with Left from any row or with Back
- Back goes back a screen, then the menu, then For you, then asks before exiting
- Focus always returns to where you were; options, audio and subtitles open in a side panel
- Live player: the info strip shows neighbouring channels; hold Left for the full list; number keys jump to a channel

**Open source**
- Relay is now open source under the GPL-3.0: https://github.com/Beechey/relay
- Updates now come from that repository. This version moves your app over automatically.

Already on an earlier version? The app offers this update itself — choose **Install now** when asked.
