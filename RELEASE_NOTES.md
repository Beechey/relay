# Relay IPTV 0.1.5

- **Full TV guide:** the guide now uses your provider's complete XMLTV listings. Move earlier or later through the day, and it refreshes in the background
- **Search everywhere:** a search box in Live TV, Movies and Series finds channels and titles inside the category you're in
- **Recents:** Home has rows for recent channels, recently watched movies and recently watched series, all in the same card style. Live TV gets a *Recent* group, and Movies and Series a *Recently watched* category
- **Continue watching, smarter:** finish an episode and the next one waits as *Up next*. Hold OK (or press and hold) to remove anything
- **Episode list in the player:** jump straight to any episode, or to another season, without leaving the video
- **Audio, subtitles and quality:** pick the track or video quality for each video when the stream offers more than one
- **Dolby and DTS audio:** streams with Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, DTS or TrueHD sound now play on devices that can't decode it themselves
- **Hide categories:** hide whole categories from Live TV, Movies and Series. In Settings you can filter by several words at once, choose *Containing* or *Not containing*, and hide or show everything listed in one go
- **Playlist refresh schedule:** every start, daily, weekly, monthly or manually
- **More settings:** live TV start-up, guide refresh, preferred audio and subtitle languages, skip lengths, 24-hour clock and more
- **Easier navigation:** movie and series pages keep the side menu (or bottom tabs on phones) and have a Back button
- **Clearer errors:** when a channel won't play, Relay says why, for example a 4K Dolby Vision stream this device can't decode, or an event channel that's off air
- **Fixes:** audio no longer carries on after leaving a video; switching channels no longer shows the old picture behind the new one; the next-episode card shows the full title and countdown; holding OK in fullscreen opens the channel menu

Already on an earlier version? The app offers this update itself — choose **Install now** when asked.
