# Relay IPTV 0.1.4

- **Picture-in-picture on phones and tablets:** while a channel, movie or episode is playing, swipe home, press Home, or tap the new picture-in-picture button in the player, and it keeps playing in a small floating window. On Android 12 and later the swipe-home gesture animates straight into the window. Tap the window to come back
- **Nothing cut off at the bottom:** the last item in the category panel and other long lists now scrolls fully into view

Already on an earlier version? The app offers this update itself — choose **Install now** when asked.
