# Relay IPTV 0.1.9

- **Cast live TV to a Chromecast:** many IPTV providers send live channels in a form Chromecast refuses. Relay now prepares the channel on your phone and passes it to the TV over your Wi-Fi, so live channels play on the Chromecast. It takes about 10 seconds to start; the phone shows "Getting the channel ready for your TV…" meanwhile
- Keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the Chromecast and Relay open (it can be in the background) while you watch

Already on an earlier version? The app offers this update itself — choose **Install now** when asked.
