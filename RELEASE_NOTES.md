# Relay IPTV 0.3.0 beta 2

Second beta of the redesign. Only installs with **Beta updates** switched on (Settings › About and updates) are offered it.

**New since beta 1**
- **For you on phones** is redesigned: a big card for the channel you were last watching with your other channels under it, Continue watching, recently watched and added, and your categories. A new playlist never opens on an empty page
- **Players** look like proper Material players on phone and TV: a clean overlay with the programme, a progress bar and real buttons (pause, from the start, go live, audio & subtitles, save, channels), instead of a panel of key hints
- **Buffering** now shows a loading indicator on the video
- **Animations** throughout: posters grow into their pages, artwork fades in, lists cascade in, cards respond to a press, overlays slide in, loading shows shimmering placeholders. Android's "Remove animations" setting is respected
- **Faster start and scrolling:** Relay now ships its own startup profile
- Movies ⇄ Series switches in place without the page flashing
- TV: Back closes the info overlay before leaving the player; Left from the channel list goes back to the group you're in

Found something odd? You can switch Beta updates off at any time; you'll then get the next stable release.
