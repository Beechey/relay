# Relay IPTV 0.3.0 beta 1

A redesign of the whole app: Material 3 Expressive on phones and tablets, Material 3 for TV on Android TV and Google TV. This is a beta — only installs with **Beta updates** switched on (Settings › About and updates) are offered it.

**Everywhere**
- New look: slate and amber colours, the Roboto Flex typeface, smooth spring animations between screens
- Every tab remembers where you were: switch away and come back to the same scroll position and category
- Faster: optimised release build, and Favourites, recents and the guide no longer slow down with very large playlists

**Phones and tablets**
- New navigation bar: For you · Live · Guide · Library · Saved (a side rail on tablets and in landscape)
- Search from the bar at the top of For you; Settings and playlists from your avatar
- Live keeps playing in a mini player while you browse
- Guide is now a list with time chips (Now, the next hours, Tonight, Tomorrow); tap a channel for its schedule
- Options open as bottom sheets; removing something offers **Undo**
- Back works the Android way, including the predictive back gesture

**Android TV**
- A menu down the left: press Left from the start of any row, or Back, to reach it
- Back goes: back a screen, then the menu, then For you, then asks before exiting
- Focus always comes back to where you were; options and audio/subtitles open in a side panel
- Live player: the info strip shows neighbouring channels; hold Left for the full channel list

Found something odd? Switch Beta updates off to go back to stable releases when the next one comes out.
