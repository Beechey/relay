# Relay IPTV 0.1.9

- **Cast live TV to a Chromecast:** many IPTV providers send live channels in a form Chromecast refuses. Relay now prepares the channel on your phone and passes it to the TV over your Wi-Fi, so live channels play on the Chromecast
- **Smooth on the TV:** the TV gets about 18 seconds of picture buffered before it starts, so short hiccups from your provider don't show. Starting takes 10–20 seconds; the phone shows "Getting the channel ready for your TV…" meanwhile
- While casting live TV, a "Casting to your TV" notification keeps the phone relaying at full speed, even with the screen off. Keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the Chromecast

Already on an earlier version? The app offers this update itself — choose **Install now** when asked.
