# Relay IPTV 0.1.7

- **Chromecast:** on phones and tablets, the Cast icon appears in the channel and movie players whenever a Chromecast is on your Wi-Fi. Pick your TV and the video moves there, carrying on from where it was; play, pause, seek and channel changes control the TV. Stop casting and it continues on your phone
- Live channels are sent to the Chromecast as HLS. If a channel won't cast, your provider may not allow that format; MKV movies can also fail depending on their codec

Already on an earlier version? The app offers this update itself — choose **Install now** when asked.
